Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order pinot gris online?

Ordering pinot gris online is generally easy and convenient. Simply search for a reputable online retailer, browse their selection of pinot gris wines, and add your desired bottle or bottles to your cart. From there, you can complete the checkout process and have the wine delivered directly to your door.

 

How much does pinot gris cost?

The cost of pinot gris can vary widely depending on the specific variety, producer, and region of origin. Generally, pinot gris wines tend to be more affordable than some other white wine varieties, but prices can still range from around $10 to $50 or more per bottle.

 

Can I order pinot gris for delivery?

Yes, many online retailers and wine shops offer delivery options for pinot gris and other types of wine. Simply check to see if the retailer you are considering offers delivery to your area, and choose the appropriate delivery option during the checkout process.

How do I store pinot gris wine?

Pinot gris wine should be stored in a cool, dark place with consistent temperature. A wine fridge or wine cellar is ideal, but a cool, dark closet or basement can also work. Pinot gris should be stored on its side to keep the cork moist, which helps prevent it from drying out and allowing air to enter the bottle.

 

How do I serve pinot gris wine?

Pinot gris is typically served chilled, at a temperature of around 50-55 degrees Fahrenheit. It can be served in a white wine glass or a broader, more rounded glass known as a Burgundy glass.

 

What food pairs well with pinot gris?

Pinot gris is a versatile wine that pairs well with a wide range of foods, including seafood, chicken, pork, and lighter pasta dishes. It can also be enjoyed on its own as an aperitif.
